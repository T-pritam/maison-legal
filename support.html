Account deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app: open Profile → Delete My Account and confirm. Your account is deactivated immediately and your personal identifiers are removed or anonymised within 30 days, as described in our Privacy Policy. If you cannot access the app, email us at the address above from your registered email and we will process your request.
