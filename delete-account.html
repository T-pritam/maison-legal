Delete Your Account

Applies to MdB Admin and MdB Trainer — the staff apps for Maison de Build.

Staff accounts (Admin, Super Admin, Trainer) are provisioned by the club — there is no public sign-up, so deletion is handled by request rather than a self-serve button in the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email employee07007@gmail.com from any address, or call/WhatsApp +91 90148 01491.
  2. Include your registered mobile number and which app you use (MdB Admin or MdB Trainer), so we can locate your account.
  3. We confirm the request and deactivate your account, typically within 2 business days.

Alternatively, a club Super Admin can remove a staff account directly from the Admin app's staff management screens on your behalf.

What gets deleted, and when

  • Your account is deactivated immediately — you can no longer sign in.
  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, profile photo) are removed or anonymised within 30 days.
  • Where required for an active dispute, fraud prevention, or a legal obligation — for example, chat records tied to an open complaint — that specific data is retained no longer than 180 days, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Records your role required for gym operations (e.g. a trainer's historical session logs with a member) may be retained in anonymised or aggregated form after your identifiers are removed, so member history stays intact.

Data types this covers

Name, email, phone number, profile photo, chat messages, and app activity associated with your staff account, as described in our Privacy Policy.
